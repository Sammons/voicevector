organization with **fewer than 1,000 employees and contractors** — under the

no license keys, and no telemetry: the software is identical for everyone.

Organizations with **1,000 or more** employees and contractors (counted
together with affiliates) need a commercial license:

| | |
|---|---|

| **Seat** | One person who uses VoiceVector in the course of their work for the organization |

| **Scope** | Both apps (macOS and Windows), all versions released during the term, and continued use of those versions after it |
| **Support** | Best-effort by email; bug reports and feature requests are welcome on GitHub |

Site licenses (a flat annual price for unlimited seats) are available for
larger deployments — ask.

## How to buy

Email **sales@sammons.io** with the organization's name, the number of seats,
and a billing contact. You'll get an invoice from Sammons Software LLC and a
signed license agreement covering the terms above. Standard vendor-onboarding paperwork (W-9,
security questionnaires) is fine.

## Why this model

VoiceVector is built and maintained by a one-person company. Small teams and
individuals use it for free; large organizations that get value from it
fund its continued development. Because there is no telemetry or license
check in the software, compliance rests with the organization — the same
way it does for any other license in your software inventory.
