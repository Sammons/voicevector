Privacy

VoiceVector doesn't collect any telemetry, cookies, or data.

The app talks directly to the AI providers you configure — your dictations go from your machine to them, and back. Those interactions are governed by the terms and privacy policies of the providers you choose. VoiceVector itself collects no data: no analytics, no crash reporting, no account, no tracking.

This website serves static pages only — no cookies, no analytics.
